# 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script: CallScreen AI

### Pitch (0:00 - 0:30)
- "High-volume frontline hiring receives hundreds of applicants. Recruiters spend 60% of their workday dialing phone numbers just to check basic shift availability, location, and salary expectations."
- "Meet CallScreen AI — an AI phone screening layer powered by CALL-E that automates repetitive phone screening and delivers structured candidate scorecards."

### Workflow Demo (0:30 - 2:15)
1. **Dashboard Overview**:
   - Show 50 warehouse applicants in queue.
   - Highlight metrics: Completed calls, Qualified candidates, Pending reviews.
2. **Job & Questions**:
   - Navigate to "Warehouse Staff (Malang)" job opening.
   - Show the 5 core screening questions (Role confirm, Experience, Location, Shift availability, Salary expectation).
3. **Trigger Screening Call (Live / Sandbox)**:
   - Select candidate (e.g., Andi Pratama, +62...).
   - Open safety confirmation modal: preview candidate name, phone number, and prompt intent.
   - Click "Start AI Screening".
4. **The Live Phone Call**:
   - Candidate receives the phone call via CALL-E.
   - AI introduces itself, conducts 2-way conversation, records responses.
5. **Real-time Scorecard Extraction**:
   - Call status updates to `Completed`.
   - Open candidate scorecard: show verbatim transcript and extracted criteria:
     - Shift: *Available for Morning & Night shifts (Match)*
     - Location: *Lives in Malang (15 mins away)*
     - Salary: *Expects Rp3.5M (Within budget)*
     - Screening Status: *Qualified*

### Impact & Wrap-up (2:15 - 3:00)
- "With CallScreen AI, recruiters review pre-screened, structured results in seconds instead of spending all day dialing phones. The human recruiter makes the final call, empowered by CALL-E."
